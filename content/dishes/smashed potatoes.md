# Ingredients
- [ ] baby potatoes
- [ ] cooking oil
- [ ] salt
- [ ] pepper
- [ ] garlic powder
- [ ] your choice of dried herbs
# Procedures
1. wash baby potatoes thoroughly (we're not peeling them)
2. preheat oven to 400F
3. boil potatoes until fork tender
4. drain and lay out on a baking sheet
5. smash with the bottom of a glass and season to taste with all the ingredients
6. bake for 10 minutes, flip, and another 10 minutes
