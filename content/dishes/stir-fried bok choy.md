# Ingredients
- [ ] bok choy
- [ ] oil
- [ ] minced garlic
- [ ] soy sauce
- [ ] salt
# Procedures
1. cut the stem off the bok choy to easily disassemble the leaves
2. soak and wash the leaves, be careful of bugs and soil
3. heat pan, and add oil when dry
4. drop bok choy in pan, and stir fry until bright green
5. add minced garlic
6. deglaze with soy sauce
7. salt to taste
