# Ingredients
- [ ] carrots
- [ ] brown sugar

# Procedures
1. boil carrots until fork tender
2. drain
3. on low-medium heat, add butter and sugar and mix until coating carrots
# Notes
* for butter, if unsure, add one tablespoon at a time
* butter to brown sugar ratio is also about 1:1
