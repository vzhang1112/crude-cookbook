# Ingredients
- [ ] red-skinned peanuts
- [ ] oil
- [ ] salt
# Procedures
1. heat on medium-high heat
2. drop peanuts, then reduce heat to medium-low
3. slowly cook until peanuts are golden brown
4. remove peanuts from pan, and season with salt while hot
